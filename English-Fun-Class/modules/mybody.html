Teacher Guide

Target Vocabulary

  • head
  • eyes
  • ears
  • nose
  • mouth
  • hands
  • legs

Target Expressions

  • What is this?
  • This is my eye.
  • These are my ears.
  • I have two ears.

Classroom English

  • Look.
  • Listen.
  • Repeat after me.
  • Point to...
  • Show me...
  • Touch your...
  • Raise your hands.

Teaching Strategy

Model vocabulary, use TPR, play Teacher Says, move to Look and Say, then Draw and Describe, formative check, and reflection.
