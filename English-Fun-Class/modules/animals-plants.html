Teacher Guide

Animal Vocabulary

  • cat, dog, bird, fish
  • rabbit, chicken, cow, duck

Plant Vocabulary

  • tree, flower, plant, leaf
  • roots, stem, leaves, flower
  • water, sunlight, air, soil

Target Expressions

  • What is it?
  • It is a cat.
  • What animal do you like?
  • I like rabbits.
  • Plants need water.

Suggested Flow

Model vocabulary, use listen-and-repeat, move to simple speaking, play Guess the Animal and Animal or Plant, then complete the quiz and reflection.
