Teacher Guide

Target Language

  • What's your name?
  • My name is ...
  • Can you spell it?
  • Nice to meet you.
  • How old are you?
  • I'm ... years old.

Classroom English

  • Listen carefully.
  • Repeat after me.
  • Look at the picture.
  • Spell your name.
  • Work in pairs.
  • Ask your friend.
  • Your turn!
  • Good job!

Suggested Flow

Model alphabet and numbers, demonstrate the dialogue, let students practice in pairs, reduce visual support, then check learning.

Classroom Tip

Start with choral repetition. Move to pair work. Let confident students answer first, then gradually reduce prompts.
