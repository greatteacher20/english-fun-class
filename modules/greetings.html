Teacher Guide

Target Language

  • Hello / Hi
  • Good morning / afternoon / evening
  • How are you today?
  • I'm fine / happy / sad.
  • Goodbye / Bye / See you / Good night

Classroom English

  • Listen carefully!
  • Repeat after me!
  • Look at the picture!
  • Work in pairs!
  • Your turn!
  • Good job!

Suggested Flow

Model → guided response → pair practice → quick check → reflection.

Classroom Tip

Use gestures and visual cues. Accept simple verbal or nonverbal responses before asking for a full sentence.
