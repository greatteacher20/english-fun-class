Teacher Guide

Target Vocabulary

  • reading, drawing, singing, playing games, playing football
  • swimming, cycling, dancing
  • classroom, library, school garden, playground

Target Expressions

  • My hobby is ...
  • I like ... in the ...
  • What is your hobby?
  • Do you like ...?
  • Yes, I do. / No, I don't.

Classroom English

  • Listen and repeat after me!
  • Look at the comic strip!
  • Practice with your partner!
  • Let's play Charades!
  • Guess the action!
  • It's your turn!
  • Ask your partner: Do you like...?

Teaching Strategy

Model the language, use listen-and-repeat, pair practice, Charades, guided role-play, TPR, peer interaction, formative assessment, and reflection.
