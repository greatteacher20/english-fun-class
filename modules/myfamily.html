Teacher Guide

Target Vocabulary

  • Father, Mother, Brother, Sister
  • Grandfather, Grandmother
  • Uncle, Aunt, Cousin

Sentence Patterns

  • Who is he?
  • He is my father.
  • Who is she?
  • She is my mother.
  • This is my brother/sister.

Classroom English

  • Look at the picture!
  • Repeat after me.
  • Listen and circle.
  • Point and speak.
  • Good afternoon, everyone!

Suggested Flow

Use picture prompts and family tree modelling, choral repetition, listening check, pair speaking, then formative assessment and reflection.
